About the AI

The offline demo uses deterministic simulated suggestions, clearly labelled. Optional live providers are currently a safe fallback, not a connected model. AI may suggest or rephrase; the rule-based engine alone scores and selects questions. In live mode, text and photos could be sent to the configured provider. AI is off by default.

Why one question?

A shorter assessment can still capture useful evidence. AquaAI asks for the highest-value missing detail, then stops when it has enough.
